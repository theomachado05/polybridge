# Expected-gap model: out-of-sample accuracy (R2)

Pre-registered in `research/gap_model/METHOD.md` (committed before any statistic was computed). Generated by `python -m gap_model.run` from saved closure tables; no data was fetched.

## Bottom line

**Verdict (pre-set rule): Accurate out of sample.** Fitting each market's rate only on closures that ended before the test closure began, the predicted SPY open gap had the right sign in 141 of 235 (60.0%), binomial p = 0.003. The slope of realized on predicted gap was +1.25 (HC3 t +2.76, permutation p < 0.001, R² 0.052, n 327).

- Criterion (i), sign accuracy > 50% with p < 0.05: **met**.
- Criterion (ii), slope > 0 with permutation p < 0.05: **met**.
- Calibration: a perfectly calibrated model has slope 1; HC3 t of (slope − 1) = +0.56.
- Error size: out-of-sample R² vs a zero forecast +0.050, vs the expanding mean gap +0.049. Most of the open gap is not explained by the PM move; the expected gap is a direction-and-size hint with a wide band, not a point forecast.
- Nominal 80% band: realized gap inside the band in 86.9% of 327 test closures (own rate 87.8%, n 287; pooled fallback 80.0%, n 40).
- Where the skill comes from (same tests, per subset; not in the verdict): election market: sign 52.4% of 84 (p = 0.744), slope +0.16 (permutation p = 0.941) -> not accurate out of sample; recession market: sign 64.2% of 151 (p < 0.001), slope +1.28 (permutation p < 0.001) -> accurate out of sample; own rate: sign 61.4% of 215 (p = 0.001), slope +1.33 (permutation p < 0.001) -> accurate out of sample; pooled rate: sign 45.0% of 20 (p = 0.824), slope -6.34 (permutation p = 0.030) -> not accurate out of sample.
- **Replication panel (10 new rule-selected markets): not accurate out of sample** — sign 50.2% of 878 (p = 0.919), slope -0.23 (date permutation p = 0.203); 0 of 10 markets pass the rule on their own.
- Product reading: the pooled rate over all 12 markets is +0.81 bp/pp (SE 0.55) with a between-market SD of 4.23 bp/pp, so for a market without its own rate the rate band spans both signs. The expected gap is informative only where a market's own rate is well determined; the UI must show the band and the closure count.

## Design

- Panel: the 380 unselected closures of `leadlag_closed` (Trump 2024 market, 149 closures; US recession 2025 market, 231). Rate = least squares through the origin (bp of SPY gap per pp of oriented PM move), HC3 SE.
- Expanding window: a closure's training set is every closure whose open was on or before its close day. Per-market rate once the market has ≥ 20 non-zero-move training closures; pooled rate before that; no prediction until the pooled set has 20 (burn-in).
- Test closures: 327 (rate source: own 287, pooled 40).

## Primary result (SPY, the mapped ETF of both markets)

- G1 sign accuracy (pred ≠ 0, gap ≠ 0): 141 of 235 (60.0%), binomial p = 0.003.
- G2 slope of realized on predicted: +1.25 (HC3 t +2.76, permutation p < 0.001, R² 0.052, n 327).
- G1 at |PM move| ≥ 1 pp: 80 of 130 (61.5%), binomial p = 0.011.

## Caveats

- **Known panel.** The full-sample relation on this panel (+7.52 bp/pp with intercept) was known before this test was designed. The expanding window never uses future closures, but the model form was chosen knowing it.
- **Two markets, two regimes.** The recession market's first closures are predicted with the election market's rate (a different market in a different year), which is exactly the product's situation for a new market.
- **Co-movement, not lead.** The PM move and the gap span the same closure; futures and pre-market SPY are not observed. An accurate expected gap does not mean the gap is capturable.
- **Serial dependence.** Consecutive closures of one market are not independent; binomial and HC3 inference are somewhat optimistic. The permutation p is the primary slope inference.
- **Many zero moves.** Closures with no PM move get a predicted gap of 0 and are outside the sign test.
