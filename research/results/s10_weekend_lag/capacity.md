# S10 capacity

- **Size at the best price on a weekend:** S9 measured the live books of Sat 2026-10-03 22:13 New York time: crude oil a median of **$20** at the best price across 7 markets priced 10% to 90% (from $1 to $256), spread 1.0 point. Spring 2026's oil markets were much larger; their books are not published.
- **The trade as run:** 100 contracts per market, at most 5 markets per signal and 3 signals per weekend; 421 trades on 31 weekends; $51 of capital per trade on average; capital base $1,027.
- **Prints behind the entries:** 17 of 421 primary entries (4%) have a public print within five minutes after the signal at the assumed price or better, on the side that proves the fill; median printed size 67 shares.
- **Stale prices:** 68% of primary entries are in a market whose one-minute price did not change in the 15 minutes before the entry.
- The trade does not pay after costs, so capacity is not the binding question.
