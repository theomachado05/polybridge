# S22 capacity

All figures are for the primary rule (quotes 5 points outside the options band), over the whole five-month window.

- Fills: 74 on 27 resolution dates in 46 markets (2.7 fills per date that had one).
- Contracts filled: 2,385 (each fill is the print's size, capped at 100).
- Filled notional at our quote price: $842.79.
- Cash locked (price for a purchase, 1 minus price for a sale): $1,036.54 in total; the most locked for one resolution date was $181.19.
- Median print size among the fills: 12.19 contracts (mean 206.02, largest 5,000.00); 15 of 74 prints were larger than our 100 and were capped.
- Median print size over all 970 evaluated prints: 12.12 contracts.
- Dollar P&L of the book: $+623.97.

This is a ceiling set by what takers printed, and a low one. The file keeps only the first buy and the first sell print of each market in each clock minute, so true printed volume is somewhat larger than counted here. With our quote present, takers might have traded more (a better price) or differently; that cannot be read from prints.
