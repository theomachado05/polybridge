# S21 capacity

- **Printed size behind B0:** a median of 79 contracts sold into bids per market over its first weekend (quartiles 24 to 465); in dollars of premium, a median of $33 per market.
- **The book as run:** up to 100 contracts per market, 60 markets, a capital base of $752, $37 of capital per market on average, locked a median of 28 days; book P&L +$1,098.
- **The ceiling the prints prove:** every contract takers sold into bids in B0's markets over those weekends: 30,690 contracts, $12,844 of premium; held to the result those sales made +$11,223. They were other people's sales: a newcomer hitting the same bids would have moved them.
- This is a trade of tens of dollars per market as run, about ten thousand dollars a year at the ceiling. The prints show what did trade at these prices, not what else could have been sold; a taker's sale needs a bid.
- The option side is not traded, so listed-option liquidity does not limit the book; it limits only how precisely the anchor is known (median band 6.0 points on the finish-beyond probability).
