# CX2 result: acquisition failure hedge cannot be tested from this dataset

**NOT_TESTABLE.** Of 892 cached event candidates, 6 ask about a corporate acquisition. All 6 settle on an announced agreement rather than completed acquisition. There are **0 eligible completion contracts, 0 verified trades and 0 recent daily observations**. Sharpe, returns, drawdown and capacity are unavailable at both 1x and 2x costs. No equity curve is fabricated.

The decisive mismatch is contractual. [The company basket](https://polymarket.com/event/which-companies-will-be-acquired-before-2027) and [eBay/GameStop](https://polymarket.com/event/will-gamestop-acquire-ebay) pay Yes for an announcement even if the deal never completes. Buying their No tokens after a signed deal would not protect against subsequent merger failure. Current official Gamma descriptions are archived in current_rules.json; their updates are from 2026-10-04 and do not prove earlier rule versions.

The eBay case also fails the cash-deal requirement: its issuer's [2026-05-04 announcement](https://investors.ebayinc.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/eBay-Confirms-Receipt-of-Unsolicited-Proposal-from-GameStop/default.aspx) describes an unsolicited non-binding proposal and stock consideration. Perplexity is a private target. No cached equity quote history for the five listed targets, historical event books, matched puts or deal-specific OCC deliverables was verified. Five preselected historical deal names produced no verified contract match in this limited discovery; this is not a claim that no such market ever existed.

**What was tested:** one strategy was preregistered at commit 01bce5402df4e76d794d906739d57760336d263d before any new metadata request. The recent 20% validation is explicitly exposed exploratory data. The price stage was stopped at semantic rejection: 0 historical price requests, no performance variants run. The audit is reproducible with `.venv/bin/python -m cx2_merger_bridge.run` from research/. The evaluator implements common target exposure, full collateral and idle cash, quote age/depth checks, observed spread/fee stresses, dividends, independent leg marks/settlements, deal clustering and daily block intervals. It requires authenticated normalized inputs before any performance report.

**25 tests passed** using `.venv/bin/python -m pytest cx2_merger_bridge/tests -q`. They check announcement versus completion, the inclusive deadline and late close, severe stock failure, revised consideration, distributions, option cash deliverables, stale/future/crossed quotes, insufficient depth, cash days, entry costs, and repeated collateral. Fixtures are mechanics illustrations only. Synthetic_truth_table.csv is separate from the empty trades.csv and equity.csv.

A useful lesson survives the null. In the fixture, 100 target shares, a $100 original offer, $80 planning strike and 2,000 No tokens produce $9,000 terminal assets at a $70 failed stock price, but only $3,000 if the stock falls to $10. A standard $80 protective put instead has $8,000 terminal assets in both states, before initial premiums. A fixed binary hedge cannot establish a stock floor. [OCC mechanics](https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/splits-mergers-spinoffs-bankruptcies) also require cash-merger option adjustments, rather than an assumed live standard put after closing.

Next evidence: a named signed cash deal with a genuine completion contract; archived contract versions known at entry; simultaneous historical equity/event/put books; dividends, failure prices and independent settlement evidence. INPUTS.md specifies the normalized capture. The frozen success threshold still requires 30 independent entry dates, at least 60 recent daily observations, Sharpe >=1.5, positive 2x results and positive uncertainty bounds. This bounded universe cannot establish that threshold. No live trade or recorder change is proposed.
