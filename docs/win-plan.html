The one-sentence thesis, as judges will hear it

Markets put a price on events before they happen. Prediction markets price the probability; the options chain prices the move. PolyBridge measures where that price is systematically wrong for a class of events and picks the hedge accordingly. Our evidence is 8-K filings × options (Massive) and a hedged portfolio (Webull). The live product is the C++20 engine that applies the same rule to prediction markets.

Prediction–Price Parity, restated for the options market: the implied move at t_pre plays the role of the market's p. The parity gap is |realized| ÷ implied, and it decides between buying protection and selling it.

6 · Guardrails and open decisions

Score killers we design out

  • Notebook needs anything but the Massive key → cap 4. No Claude, no C++ build in the scored path.
  • Lookahead: post-bell filings traded at that day's close → EDGAR acceptance fix, signals lagged.
  • Touching OOS, then changing things → OOS runs once, after the method freeze, logged in git.
  • Reporting the best of N → every variant counted, deflated Sharpe, nearby parameters shown.
  • Gross numbers → every result net, plus costs doubled.
  • Keys, .env, .massive_cache or licensed data committed → gitignored, checked before going public.
  • A note without code, or code without a note → both on Devpost, repo public by 10 AM.
