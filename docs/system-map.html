How a prediction-market tick becomes an equity hedge

Prediction markets publish a live probability for an event. PolyBridge reads that probability, converts it into dollar exposure on the stocks the event moves, and keeps an equity hedge sized to it. The trading loop runs in C++. Everything slow stays in Python.

2 · Starting position: Prediction–Price Parity

The stock already prices in the event at the market probability: S = p·S_yes + (1−p)·S_no. The hedge sizes from the part of the move that is not priced in yet. Example: 1,200 ABNB, market at 23¢, AI estimates the event gap J at 3.2%.

Confirmed: PPP = Prediction–Price Parity. The impact model returns J; the engine sizes from the remaining exposure and trades on the parity gap. Exact sizing rules come in the next spec revision.
